## Users

Primary: owners and decision makers of Indian businesses and institutes who need software built or a ready product deployed: college and training-institute heads, school principals and administrators, retail and boutique owners, bakery and cafe owners, and founders or managers commissioning a custom web, mobile or AI build. They arrive to judge whether Edgevyn can be trusted with their system, and they want to see real working software, not promises.

## Product Purpose

Edgevyn is a software company that builds scalable, secure, high-performance software for any business, from early-stage MVPs to enterprise-grade systems. The landing page exists to show that range through five live products and to get the visitor to book a guided demo or a call about a custom build. Success: a qualified visitor books a demo or call.

## Positioning

Edgevyn shows its work as live software anyone can open today: five shipped products across education, school administration, cafe planning, fashion retail and bakery commerce, each with a working demo. Most agencies show screenshots and logos; Edgevyn shows running systems built for how Indian businesses actually operate (Tamil and English, UPI and COD, WhatsApp, same-day local delivery).

## Product Principles

1. Show running software, not claims: every capability on the page points to something a visitor can open.
2. Built for how Indian businesses really work: local languages, local payments, WhatsApp, local delivery.
3. One clear next step: every section leads toward booking a demo or call.
4. Honest and specific: concrete features and named products over adjectives; nothing invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visitors use phones on variable networks as often as office desktops; the page must stay fast and fully usable at phone width. Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
